**Barrel Rivals — complete-product build plan**  
Engineering revision 5 · Gameplay revision 4 · Original 53 sections · Solo creator with AI-assisted development

This is the implementation plan for the complete Barrel Rivals game. It preserves your exact eight category ranges and 53 section titles. `Barrel-Rivals-Gameplay-Blueprint.md` defines player behavior and rules; `Barrel-Rivals-Design-Decisions.md` records choices and experiments. Engineering revision 5 applies [the ten-mechanic Reins integration](Reins-Mechanics-Integration.md) and [decision record](../Decisions/Reins-Lab-Gameplay.md) across the existing section cards. These current contracts supersede drawing-only requirements for the opt-in Lab while preserving the 0.3.0 Classic checkpoint, its section aliases and rules version 1.

**Full product scope.** Live 1v1 turn-based competition is the lead mode: one rider runs while the other spectates. Recorded challenges and simultaneous racing remain required by your earlier request. The game includes at least ten distinct horses, horse upgrades and customization, gear, earned arena tiers, currencies, rewards and a premium store. A short third-person introduction transitions into first-person riding. Classic retains launch release/drawing/automatic riding. The new Reins Lab develops rein steering, cadence, geometric pockets/kisses, three-tap Gate Break, contextual Leg Wrap, Dirt Read, alternating Drive, bounded Horse IQ/bond/stamina, fair compatible ghosts and capped earned streak rewards. Default pending user preference: additive Lab, earned-only bonuses with no existing-winnings loss or premium insurance. All percentages remain provisional; no live service or economy is implied.

**Current Reins boundary.** The source uses side rein pads and an active center cadence/Gate/Wrap pad; real two-thumb comfort remains a phone test gate. Bounded Core horse parameters and local own-best playback do not complete Horse IQ/bonding or rival competition. Horse picker/bond/streak preview UI, learned behavior, rival gaps and online settlement remain planned. Current prototype dimensions/cadence are provisional; the scope and acceptance columns below describe work to qualify, not completed features.
