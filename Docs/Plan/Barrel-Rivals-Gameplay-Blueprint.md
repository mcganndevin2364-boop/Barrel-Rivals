**Barrel Rivals — complete-game blueprint**  
Gameplay revision 4 · Engineering revision 5 · Original eight categories / 53 sections

**The outcome.** A polished iOS/Android arcade barrel-racing game with skillful riding, horses worth developing and trustworthy competition. The new **Reins Lab** explores two-thumb reins, rhythmic cadence, geometric barrel pockets, active wrap and alternating home-stretch Drive. It retains the rodeo course, rider bodycam and short third-person introduction. Read [the Reins decision](../Decisions/Reins-Lab-Gameplay.md) and [all ten integration contracts](Reins-Mechanics-Integration.md) alongside this blueprint.

**Scope and assumptions.** The user requested all ten mechanics in the attached redesign. Pending optional preference answers, build an additive opt-in Lab and use capped earned streak bonuses, with no loss of existing winnings or premium loss insurance. These are working recommendations to test, not final approval of permanent replacement or economy tuning. Preserve the working 0.3.0 **Classic Practice** checkpoint with its liked hold/release, remembered drawing, automatic path and exit tap. The new ruleset supersedes drawing-only requirements for the Lab; it does not silently change Classic.

The complete product still includes live turns/spectating, recorded challenges, simultaneous competition, at least ten distinct horses, five earned arena tiers/presentations, horse/gear progression, career/recovery, coins/diamonds/trophies, missions/seasons and a tested store. The 53 original section IDs/titles remain traceable aliases. No online service/database or monetization is deployed by this design. Development art is original or free with verified commercial-use rights; no paid asset purchase is authorized.

**The reason to return.** Each attempt should explain one skill improvement and a next voluntary challenge. Test clarity, mastery, rematches, frustration and fatigue. The attachment's claims about addiction or gambling psychology are not measured results and are not success criteria. Short matches, honest comparisons and accessible controls must stand on their own.

**A Reins run with visible cause and effect.**

| Phase | Player action | Rules / ownership |
|---|---|---|
| Arena preview | Read footing, route and frozen horse/trait/loadout profile. | Versioned per-round surface map; both competitors receive the same map and preview opportunity. |
| Gate Break | Tap three energy-wave peaks on the center pad. | Graded launch and bounded false-break recovery. The Lab adopts three taps despite the attachment's hold/release heading. No artificial negative time credit. |
| Alley / Cadence | Drag a side rein to steer; tap center with the free thumb. Both side reins brake. | One cadence event at center press; held input does not repeat beats. Bounded cadence gain and Hot/Blazing duration, not indefinite stacking. |
| Barrel Pocket | Choose a wide line or a narrow clean pass. | Horse-to-barrel **surface clearance** and legal route progress decide contact/zone outcome. No hidden knock probability. |
| Leg Wrap | Hold center for 300 ms near a barrel; the other thumb steers a side rein. | The earlier press supplies one cadence edge; later Wrap trades future cadence opportunity/speed for bounded turn control. Flash Wrap remains a tuning hypothesis, not collision immunity. |
| Exit / next barrel | Recover cadence and steer to the next legal barrel. | One earned physical exit boost per barrel; no separate inherited drawing-exit tap unless a later Lab decision explicitly adds it. |
| Home Stretch Drive | After legal barrel three, alternate left/right taps. | Capped attainable gain; same-side spam does not help; no bonus after the finish. |

**All ten mechanics and their limits.**

1. **Reins Tension:** left/right normalized tension controls steering, combined tension braking. Light/medium/full pull and counter-rein curves must be tuned together with speed, grip and horse bounds. The proposed 8/15/35% speed costs are starting hypotheses.
2. **Cadence Gallop:** grade center presses against a visible/audio beat schedule with one acceptance per opportunity. Current Core uses 300–500 ms periods (2–3.33 Hz), ±40/80/140 ms windows and 5/10-perfect streaks. These provisional values and boosts require real-phone timing/accessibility tests. A miss has a bounded recovery state; purchase, opponent lead and framerate cannot widen windows.
3. **Barrel Pocket / Kiss:** Safe/Risk/Pocket/Kiss describe graduated clearance/line opportunities, not random bets. Authored collision hull plus barrel dimensions determine clean clearance, grazing/contact and one +5-second knock. Do not apply the source's center-distance bands directly to every horse. Style points are separate from finish time. Visual wobble cannot change the accepted result independently.
4. **Gate Break:** three wave taps; explicit wrong-phase and spam behavior; bounded standstill/recovery on a false break. Improved acceleration/boost is the advantage. Never subtract the proposed 0.4/0.15 seconds from a stopwatch; do not count standstill twice.
5. **Leg Wrap:** center press creates one cadence event; continuing to hold for 300 ms near a barrel requests Wrap, with a visible speed/control tradeoff and no repeated held cadence. Test short Flash Wrap timing as a hypothesis; actual contact still counts.
6. **Dirt Read:** Hard Pack, Loose Sand, Tacky Clay, Muddy and Mixed profiles alter bounded movement/grip. Both riders share the exact round manifest; degradation occurs between rounds only. Cosmetic ruts never worsen conditions for the second rider.
7. **Home Stretch Drive:** alternating taps with a hard cap and a disclosed carryover/stamina policy. Fourteen taps per second is an extreme stress case, not the required maximum-performance default. Compare lower attainable caps and declared accessible alternatives; no lead-dependent clutch threshold changes.
8. **Horse IQ:** Nerve, Fire, Biddability and Heart are bounded, explainable movement/effort traits. Use immediate accepted input with movement response curves, not artificial 80 ms input lag. No random flinches, automated barrel steering, paid timing windows or mid-round stat drift. Core parameters exist, but the Lab has no horse picker, bond preview or learned behavior. Bond UI and explicit stamina progression are planned; later grants require trusted settlement.
9. **Rival Ghost Pressure:** optional compatible ghost, audio and visual drama. Ahead/behind does not change cue visibility, timing windows, grip or Drive thresholds. The gap compares crossing times at the same legal course progress and is marked unavailable until valid. In turn-based competition, provide equal preassigned reference information or defer the opponent's run-specific ghost until both finish; do not privilege the second rider.
10. **Streak Stakes:** retain progression through an earned-only capped bonus proposal, pending the user's choice. The original table and recursive worked example are inconsistent and would compound a pot rapidly. Freeze base reward B and award fixed milestone extras; never multiply accumulated balances. No previous-winnings loss or premium insurance. A labelled local calculator/preview is planned, not implemented; later API/DB settlement owns real awards.

**Two-thumb input ownership.** Current controls use LEFT/RIGHT drag pads plus an active CENTER cadence/Wrap pad. One thumb holds a turning rein while the other presses center for cadence or holds it for 300 ms near a barrel for Wrap. Both reins brake and temporarily occupy both thumbs; Gate taps center and Drive alternates sides. The cadence press and subsequent Wrap are intentionally separate timeline events. A pointer owns one pad until release/cancel; menu touches cannot fall through. Measure hand swapping, center reach, occlusion, tremor, both-rein braking and wrapping while steering on small/large phones. The alternative two-zone tap/drag classifier is unimplemented; compare it only if phone testing shows a need.

**Prototype limits.** Current contact uses 0.42 m + 0.40 m circular bounds and a provisional 1.0–1.2 m center-distance Kiss band. Production clearance must be reconciled with the authored horse/barrel hulls; the prototype does not prove realistic dimensions. Own-best playback exists, while rival gap UI/network, horse bonding and streak previews remain planned. Each source/test/build/device result needs separate evidence.

**Rules, clocks and replay.** Classic rules version 1 remains a drawing-only namespace. Lab records/replays require `reins-lab-v1`, schema/configuration hash, course hash, round/footing manifest, horse/class snapshot and input-mode identity; a bare integer version or shared seed is insufficient. Do not import a Classic own-best as a Lab rival. Reject incompatible/corrupt data with a clear explanation rather than silently re-score it.

The intended Lab Core experiment runs 20 ms fixed simulation steps in C#/.NET Standard 2.1. Accepted timestamps, sequences, range limits and numeric quantization are part of its contract. Same-tick ordering and stale input behavior must be explicit. Fixed stepping is not proof that floating-point implementations agree across Unity IL2CPP and a backend; conformance fixtures must demonstrate it. Unity supplies input/presentation and interpolates the accepted pose; rendering FPS, local pause and Unity physics do not author results.

For a completed Lab run, final time is **validated elapsed simulation from the published start cue to legal finish + 5 seconds for each uniquely knocked barrel**, plus only separately specified foul terms. Prefer false-break standstill within elapsed simulation, with no duplicate added penalty. Boosts change physical movement; style points never subtract time. Legal three-barrel sequence/direction, skipped barrel, boundaries, timeout, DNF and restart behavior are required Core work.

The Lab does not inherit Classic's fixed drawing slowdown by default. If later configured, slowdown belongs to an equal versioned per-rider schedule; one rider cannot alter another's clock. The trusted service must validate continuous analog samples and discrete edges with bounded timestamp/lead/lag handling. Offline deterministic replay is not anti-cheat or proof of honest hardware capture. M1N tests latency, packet loss/reordering, stale held reins, reconnect, equal surfaces, information disclosure and client/verifier disagreement before ranking.

Recorded ghost playback uses its own simulation and course-progress map. A visual lead is not automatically a time lead, especially with penalties; label the gap definition and penalty difference. Hide unavailable/overlapping ghosts, preserve readable control UI and allow cosmetic effects off without competitive changes.

**Winner and interruption rules.** Quick Duel compares one penalized time each. Championship compares the sum of three penalized times; dividing both sums by three gives the same winner. Compare the underlying stored values, not rounded HUD text. An exact tie is a draw: refund entry stakes, award no winner prize or trophy transfer, keep already consumed match items consumed, and allow a rematch. DNF is a status, not a zero time. A valid completed competitor beats a DNF. Championship requires three valid runs; a player-caused DNF or forfeit ends that player's attempt without a replacement run. Both player-caused DNFs settle as a double-loss with no winner prize and normal spent stakes/items. Confirmed service failures use the separate void/refund policy.

Reserve the match entry once before play. Consume event consumables once at the published match-start boundary, covering all Championship runs; show this duration before equipping. A cancellation before start releases reservations. A player timeout/forfeit follows the published loss policy; a service-caused void refunds entry and restores reserved/consumed match items through an idempotent correction. Reward calculations must distinguish those cases so disconnecting cannot repeatedly mint refunds. Reconnection does not rewind already accepted skill phases.

**Ten horses and a stable worth returning to.** Use ten distinct entries as the initial full-roster minimum, distributed across the five tiers already sketched in the repository. Start with two complementary horse profiles per tier as a content/balance proposal: for example, stronger acceleration versus more stable turning. Final horse identities, breeds and art require a content pass. Distinguish them through silhouette/markings, animation presentation, personality and bounded performance; coat swaps alone do not fulfill the whole collection goal.

Horse ownership, upgrade level, career state and event eligibility are separate records. A developed starter remains useful in its class; higher classes provide higher performance ceilings and fresh skill difficulty. Unlock the next event through trophies and demonstrated play, with an attainable horse-readiness requirement. Document a complete free route to every competitive class. Buying a premium horse gives ownership/progression value but does not buy earned event access.

Comparable games support meaningful equipment progression: [CSR2](https://www.zynga.com/games/csr-racing-2/) emphasizes upgrades/tuning and collection, and [8 Ball Pool](https://support.miniclip.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015644088-Victory-Cues-8-Ball-Pool) gives upgradeable cues real stats. Our working choice keeps visible horse/gear advantages inside explicit event power limits and matchmaking bands. Publish how an owned horse’s effective stats change for the selected event. The comparison motivates the model; it does not prove our eventual balance or retention.

Balance with concrete tests: identical skill inputs should expose the intended stat difference; a meaningfully cleaner run by the weaker legal loadout should be able to overcome that difference. A premium setup should not erase a normal five-second knock disadvantage against a clean comparable opponent. Exact caps and bonus percentages follow those tests. Purchases and upgrades must never change cadence/launch windows, rein capture latency, autonomous steering or ghost information to guarantee wins; Classic recognizer thresholds also remain unchanged.

**Progression, rewards and recovery.** The Lab has no live economy. Its Streak Stakes alias currently means the capped earned-only proposal in [the integration specification](Reins-Mechanics-Integration.md); existing-wallet losses and premium insurance are excluded pending the user’s choice. Bond and streak credits are granted later by idempotent trusted settlement, never from a local Lab result. Coins fund ordinary upgrades and eligible event fees; diamonds fund clearly specified premium offers; trophies are earned progression. Always offer free practice and a free-entry novice competition route that can earn legitimate recovery rewards with anti-farming limits. Check a zero-balance player and a prolonged loss streak explicitly. Required gear charges or horse recovery must not make every playable route disappear.

Use temporary gear as a visible pre-match choice with bounded stacking, declared duration and reliable consumption/refund behavior. Offer horse/gear/cosmetic packs with clear contents, an optional pass and earnable crates. Missions should reward ordinary racing, skill improvement and collection development. Keep a nearby attainable goal and longer trophy-road goals; do not make every screen demand a separate chore.

Career/aging should begin with maturity, experience and milestones. Injury/recovery remains in the full plan, with reversible consequences, preserved ownership and an available route to play. Its exact competitive effects require a prototype before activation; do not silently add irreversible retirement or paid-only recovery. Freeze the loadout and disclosed per-round condition/stamina schedule before competition; later bond/gear rewards affect a future match. Both riders follow the same footing schedule, with no order-dependent change.

For purchases, use verified backend entitlements and tested mobile-store flows. Begin with fixed-content paid packs while progression balance develops. If randomized items are sold, disclose actual odds before purchase under [Apple’s guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [Google Play’s policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en). Prices, odds, pass duration and final offer contents are open tuning/business decisions; no revenue promise is implied.

**Presentation and content standard.** Horse/rider animation, bodycam framing, hoof cadence, believable turning, dirt contact and readable skill feedback get priority. Lens distortion, chromatic effects, blur, exposure and shake are configurable polish. Keep rein/cadence/wrap/Drive controls readable and the Classic drawing surface stable; provide a comfortable reduced-motion profile with identical competitive rules. Use original/free assets with verified commercial rights and required attribution; the current choice authorizes no paid art purchase. Render one representative final-quality horse/arena on target phones early, then scale assets using measured budgets and consistent art direction.

Map the five event tiers to five coherent arena presentations; reuse production assets where appropriate while changing composition, identity and atmosphere. The standard course reference is 90 feet between the first two barrel centers, 105 feet from each to the third and a 60-foot score line. Verify layout/clearances and distinguish any shortened arcade variants. [WPRA standard-pattern description](https://wpra.com/the-proof-is-in-the-dirt-in-the-wilderness-circuit/).

Its shared C# core, Unity adapters and proposed trusted service stack implement the rules here; stack changes must preserve these player-facing contracts.

**Technical ownership.** Keep validated race rules, input grading and time calculation in a versioned simulation layer with tests. Unity handles presentation and input capture; trusted services own eligibility, challenge assignments, verified results, wallet and entitlements. Networking carries session and spectator/remote state. Photon Fusion 2 remains the intended candidate, but mobile topology, authentication, reconnect behavior and cost need a proof before commitment. Photon documents distinct trust and mobile-connectivity tradeoffs by topology. [Photon guidance](https://doc.photonengine.com/fusion/v2/fusion-choose).

Do not assume Unity physics produces identical replay results on every device. Prototype a bounded reproducible movement/scoring model or validate on an authoritative simulation. Macro input, collusion and account abuse remain detection/operation concerns even when payloads and scores are validated. Instrument rejected runs, disagreements and economic corrections with enough context to diagnose them without excessive personal data collection.

**What full completion means.** All three requested competition modes, the ten-horse roster, earned five-tier journey, gear/rewards/store, career/recovery rules, coherent arenas/bodycam/audio/UI, onboarding, safe account recovery, stable backend services and verified iOS/Android builds must pass their mapped acceptance checks. A one-horse prototype is an intermediate proof. Evidence for the complete product includes actual device builds, network-failure tests, economic recovery scenarios, representative new-player sessions and store-readiness records.
