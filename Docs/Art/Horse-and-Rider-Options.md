## September 20 isolated anatomy comparison

A second free candidate, **Horse by b2przemo**, was evaluated separately from the game. The [author listing](https://blendswap.com/blend/13903) and the included license in the [public mirror](https://github.com/nrz/ylikuutio) identify Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 and credit b2przemo. No candidate asset was added to the game or purchased. The mirrored `horse.fbx` SHA-256 is `02f48f4354edc8623a941161e3072aa87ff274ebfc2375821ca82d5ff4db56ef`.

The inspected mirror contains 326,242 vertices / 652,480 triangles, one UV layer, and no rig, actions or embedded textures. The author's original description mentions maps, but those maps are absent from this inspected mirror. Reversing four subdivision steps and applying a mild reduction produced a clean isolated 39,000-triangle candidate. A deterministic 12,000-point source-triangle sample measured 0.222 mm mean, 0.690 mm p95 and 2.832 mm maximum reduction distance at the 2.12 m comparison scale; this is not a Hausdorff bound. Direct reduction to 32,000 triangles produced visible shading defects and was rejected.

The candidate has a more believable cheek/muzzle base, but its eyes and hooves still need work. At equal full-body height it is about 25% narrower and 14% shorter than the current horse, with a different leg stance. Blind weight transfer would be inappropriate. Adoption requires deliberate skeleton fitting, textures/UV validation, eye treatment, new contact calibration and full moving Unity review. The current source therefore uses an original constrained refinement of the existing CC0 mesh; see [the surface checkpoint](Reins-Surface-Fit-Checkpoint.md). This comparison remains useful for a later deliberate replacement, not evidence that a finished replacement is installed.
