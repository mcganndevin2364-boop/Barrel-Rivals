- PracticeBuilder owns Assets/_Project/Generated/Practice. The M1 scene is offline one-barrel practice; local seeds, grades and results must not award currency/rank or be treated as trusted authority.
- The user chose Reins Racing as the main game, first-person throughout. In 0.5 remove Classic from the player-facing build/navigation; retain its source, evidence and saves as history. Current 0.4 still contains both scenes. Never import Classic or reins-lab-v1 bests into the planned reins-v2 rules/save namespace. No new project/restart is required.
- Reins scope includes dual reins, cadence, pockets/kisses, contextual Wrap, equal footing, Drive, bounded traits/bond/stamina, compatible ghosts and capped earned streak previews. The approved 0.5 launch replaces three-wave taps with a four-second/six-metre walk, three beeps at 2/3/4 seconds, and one graded hold/release at the invisible start line. Race time begins at the fixed third beep; misses grade and continue. Follow the exact v2 timing/acceleration/input contract in the 0.5 plan. All three requested multiplayer experiences and the 53 section IDs remain in scope.
- Implemented 0.4/v1 Reins input uses LEFT/RIGHT drag pads and a CENTER cadence/Wrap pad: one thumb steers, the other presses center for cadence or holds it 300 ms near a barrel for Wrap. The initial cadence edge and later Wrap are distinct timeline events; held Wrap does not generate repeated cadence. Both side drags brake; Gate taps center and Drive alternates sides. Test two-thumb reach/hand changes before accepting this layout. A two-zone tap/drag classifier is an unimplemented alternative, not the current API.
- Actual horse/barrel geometric contact decides knocks; presentation wobble and hidden RNG cannot change the result. Current circular bounds and Kiss bands are provisional prototype geometry, not verified realistic horse dimensions.
- No opponent-lead-based timing windows, noisy/harder cue UI, clutch threshold advantage, artificial horse input lag or paid auto-steering. Surface degradation is equal for both riders and occurs between rounds only. Movement boosts do not subtract arbitrary time credits.
- Default streak policy pending user preference: fixed-base capped earned bonuses, no loss of existing winnings or premium insurance. Bond/streak calculators and their UI are planned, not implemented; no Lab currency/rank/ownership awards. Current horse traits are bounded Core parameters without a Lab picker or learned behavior. Local own-best playback exists; rival gaps/network are not wired. Trusted settlement and deployment are future work.
- Sequence: preserved R0/R1 checkpoints → approved R2/0.5 rules/input, one rigged horse/rider/alley benchmark, full-course presentation and phone qualification → M1N continuous-input authority proof → online/economy/content/release. The user has played and prefers Reins; that is qualitative feedback, not measured performance or full device qualification. Do not call code, tests, native builds, device acceptance or services complete without their own evidence.
